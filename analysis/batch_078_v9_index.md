# PART A — High-Yield Preferred: Coupon보다 Funding Waterfall

## 2. Anthracite 2007 / 2008

2007:
- $25 par
- entry **$13.67**
- 8.25% coupon
- current yield ~15%

2008:
- entry ~**38% of par ≈ $9.50**
- current yield **20%+**
- author: could double if things normalize

실제:
- 2009 common + Series C/D NYSE suspension/delisting
- management: reorg/liquidation에서 shareholders **no value** 예상
- 2010-03-15 **Chapter 7**

### 핵심

Static interest coverage와 common-equity book cushion은 repo haircut, margin calls, financing withdrawal을 막지 못했다.

> **Preferred seniority is not liquidity seniority.**

# PART G — Batch 078 공통 분석식

### Leveraged Preferred

**Preferred recovery = enterprise liquidation value - all senior claims - liquidity destruction**

### Staffing

**Revenue = assignments × bill rate × duration**, not structural vacancy alone.

### Mortgage Equity

**Economic return = book-value change + dividends**, adjusted for leverage/hedges.

### Accounting Short

**Short quality = distortion × economic deterioration × catalyst**

### Vertical Software

**Strategic value = recurring revenue × retention quality × workflow/data moat × buyer synergies**

## 11. 재사용 체크리스트

1. preferred/common을 반드시 구분한다.
2. par value를 recovery floor로 쓰지 않는다.
3. repo-funded vehicle은 daily liquidity를 stress한다.
4. secular shortage와 staffing cycle을 분리한다.
5. non-GAAP critique에 recognition catalyst가 있는지 본다.
6. book growth + dividends를 함께 본다.
7. complex share actions가 있으면 official TSR을 우선한다.
8. trust preferred를 subordinated credit로 본다.
9. vertical software는 recurring mix/retention을 핵심 KPI로 둔다.
10. illiquid OTC 종목은 불확실한 historical quote로 IRR을 만들지 않는다.
